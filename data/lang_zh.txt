This is the biggest update TaterPeeler has had so far. Here's everything that's new in 2.0. Thanks for 300 players!	这是 TaterPeeler 迄今为止最大的更新。2.0 的所有新内容都在这里。感谢 300 位玩家！
Online (Beta)	在线（测试版）
You can play with other people now. Do a quick 1v1, or make a lobby with up to 6 people, public or private. The host picks the mode: race to a number of potatoes, timed, or endless. If the host leaves, the next person who joined takes over. There's quick chat, and typed chat in private lobbies (it's filtered).	现在可以和别人一起玩了。来一局快速 1v1，或者开一个最多 6 人的大厅，公开或私密都行。房主选模式：比谁先削到指定数量、限时，或无尽模式。房主离开的话，下一个加入的人接手。有快捷聊天，私密大厅里还能打字聊天（有过滤）。
Leaderboard	排行榜
There's a live leaderboard for potatoes peeled, Rush, fries and time played. Save to the cloud to get on it.	有实时排行榜，比削皮数量、Rush、薯条和游戏时长。存到云端就能上榜。
Cloud saves	云存档
Pick a 6-character code and your progress is saved online. Type the code on another device and keep playing there. You can even combine two accounts and everything adds up.	选一个 6 位代码，你的进度就会保存在线上。在另一台设备上输入代码，就能接着玩。甚至可以合并两个账号，所有数据都会加在一起。
Seasoning and TaterCoins	调味和 TaterCoins
When your fries land in the tray, grab the salt shaker and shake it over them. Not too little, not too much. You earn TaterCoins for how well you cut and seasoned, and rarer potatoes pay more.	薯条落进托盘后，拿起盐瓶在上面摇一摇。不能太少，也不能太多。切得好、调味好就能赚 TaterCoins，越稀有的土豆赚得越多。
The Shop	商店
Spend your TaterCoins in the shop (the coin button on the menu). There are 10 skins that go on any peeler, from Cherry all the way up to Diamond.	在商店里花 TaterCoins（菜单上的金币按钮）。有 10 款皮肤，任何削皮刀都能用，从樱桃一直到钻石。
Knives	刀
New knives unlock the more fries you make. Different colours and looks, all the way to a gold one.	薯条做得越多，解锁的新刀越多。不同的颜色和造型，最后还有一把金刀。
Kitchens	厨房
The game takes place in a kitchen now, with the cutting board on the counter. There are 12 kitchens to pick from in Settings, or you can use your own photo as the background.	游戏现在在厨房里进行，砧板放在台面上。设置里有 12 个厨房可选，也可以用你自己的照片做背景。
A ton of new potatoes to find, including Ube. There's also one super rare potato that looks totally normal... until you start peeling it. You'll know when you find it.	一大堆新土豆等你发现，包括 Ube。还有一个超级稀有的土豆，看起来完全正常……直到你开始削它。找到的时候你就知道了。
4 new peelers	4 把新削皮刀
Wooden, Wire Y, Pro Grip and Julienne. Each one looks different, not just a new colour. The rare ones peel a little faster, nothing crazy.	木柄、钢丝 Y、Pro Grip 和切丝刨。每把外形都不一样，不只是换个颜色。稀有的削得稍微快一点，不夸张。
Titles and trophies	称号和奖杯
Earn titles like Beginner-Peeler, Pro-Peeler and Master-Fryer, and wear one under the logo. There are trophies for time played too (only peeling and frying counts, not sitting on the menu), plus a bunch of new achievements.	赢得新手削皮师、专业削皮师、大师炸薯师等称号，选一个戴在标志下面。还有游戏时长奖杯（只算削皮和油炸，待在菜单里不算），外加一大堆新成就。
Take a photo and it gets wrapped around a potato. Find it in the Collection.	拍一张照片，它就会包在土豆上。去收藏里找它。
Left-handed mode, peeling with one finger while turning with another, your own Rush length (like 1:30), and lifetime counters for every peel and cut.	左手模式，一根手指削皮另一根手指转动，自定义 Rush 时长（比如 1:30），还有每次削皮和切割的总计数。
Every sound has its own volume slider now. And if you're listening to YouTube or music, turn on Play alongside other apps so TaterPeeler doesn't stop it.	现在每个声音都有自己的音量滑块。如果你在听 YouTube 或音乐，打开「和其他 App 一起播放」，TaterPeeler 就不会把它停掉。
Languages	语言
TaterPeeler speaks Chinese, Spanish (both kinds), Russian, Portuguese, German, Japanese, French, Korean, Polish and Turkish now, plus a Pirate language.	TaterPeeler 现在会说中文、西班牙语（两种都有）、俄语、葡萄牙语、德语、日语、法语、韩语、波兰语和土耳其语，还有海盗语。
Other stuff	其他
A Show me around button in Settings, a Suggestions box so you can send me ideas, an all-time counter, carved shapes inside the potato if you want them, and a screensaver if you leave the game alone for a while.	设置里的「带我逛逛」按钮、可以给我发想法的建议箱、总计数器、想要的话还能在土豆里刻图案，以及游戏放着不动时的屏保。
Fixes	修复
Pushing the potato off the board at the fry station doesn't break it anymore, a new one just drops in. Text is easy to read on every background, the peeler doesn't go through the cutting board, and older iPads run smoother.	在炸薯条站把土豆推下砧板不会再出问题了，会直接掉下来一个新的。文字在任何背景上都清楚易读，削皮刀不会再穿过砧板，旧款 iPad 也更流畅了。
