== 45 | Nov 23, 2026
- Settings: the build number is at the bottom now. Tap it to see this list of every fix.
- TaterMaster: quick emotes. Tap the speech bubble at the top right and pick Nice!, Hurry up!, Ha ha!, Oops!, Good game or Uh oh. Everyone at the table sees it over your head.
- TaterMaster: against bots, a bot sometimes answers your emote.
- TaterMaster: room rules for the host: strips per pass (2, 3, 4 or 5), how fast the light comes on (slow, normal, fast) and sudden death.
- TaterMaster: sudden death means that when two are left, it's one strip each and the light comes on sooner.
- TaterMaster: everyone else in the room sees the host's rules under the match.
- TaterMaster: special hot potatoes now and then (never in the first round): a tiny one, a slippery one that peels in thinner strips, and a rare golden one.
- TaterMaster: every strip you peel off a golden potato pays 5 TaterCoins.
- TaterMaster: the banner says when a round's potato is special.
- TaterMaster: "Peel 3 strips" and the strip marks follow the room's rules (and say "1 strip" in sudden death).
- Leaderboard: a TaterMaster board (games won online), all time and for this week.
- Leaderboard: This week has Potatoes and TaterMaster to pick from.
- Portfolios: a TaterMaster wins tile. On someone else's portfolio, yours shows under it.
- A game only counts toward the TaterMaster board when there are at least two players in the room, and only once per game.
